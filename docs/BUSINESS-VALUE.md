# Excelr8 Business Value

## One-Liner

Excelr8 automates lead research, campaign setup, CRM syncing, and reporting for GTM teams.

## What The Project Is About

Excelr8 is an internal AI automation dashboard built to help sales, marketing, and growth teams manage the full lead-to-campaign workflow from one place.

Instead of manually moving data between spreadsheets, LinkedIn, Airtable, HubSpot, Supabase, and n8n, Excelr8 gives the team a central dashboard for:

- Importing lead files
- Enriching and reviewing leads
- Building campaign lists
- Finding relevant LinkedIn engagement signals
- Launching outreach workflows
- Tracking campaign KPIs
- Syncing useful data into HubSpot
- Generating newsletter content

The business goal is simple: reduce manual operations work, improve lead quality, and help the team launch campaigns faster.

## What Excelr8 Does

| Feature | What It Does | Business Benefit |
|---|---|---|
| Dashboard | Shows lead totals, statuses, tiers, growth trends, and recent leads | Gives leadership and operators a quick view of pipeline health |
| File Ingestion | Uploads CSV lead files into the automation pipeline | Removes manual copy-paste work and reduces data entry errors |
| Dossiers | Centralizes lead research and profile context | Helps reps personalize outreach faster |
| Post Radar | Analyzes LinkedIn posts, commentators, reactioners, and ICP fit | Finds warmer leads based on real LinkedIn engagement |
| Campaign Manager | Creates campaigns from CSVs, normalizes lead data, links leads to campaigns, and can create Airtable/n8n assets | Reduces campaign launch time from hours to minutes |
| KPI Dashboard | Tracks campaign performance and campaign totals | Makes reporting and optimization faster |
| Newsletter | Pulls files by date and triggers newsletter generation | Speeds up recurring content creation |
| HubSpot Sync | Sends leads, campaigns, associations, dossier links, and LinkedIn activity into HubSpot | Keeps CRM data updated without manual admin work |

## How It Saves Time

Excelr8 saves time by replacing repetitive manual workflows with structured automation.

| Workflow | Manual Process Without Excelr8 | Automated Process With Excelr8 | Estimated Time Saved |
|---|---|---|---:|
| Lead import | Clean spreadsheets, rename columns, copy records between tools | Upload CSV once and route it through the pipeline | 10-20 hrs/month |
| Lead research | Manually open LinkedIn profiles, notes, and external sources | Review lead dossiers and profile context in one place | 30-130 hrs/month |
| LinkedIn prospecting | Manually inspect posts, comments, and reactions | Use Post Radar to identify relevant engaged people | 8-18 hrs/month |
| Campaign setup | Create campaign records, tables, lists, and workflows manually | Campaign Manager normalizes data and creates campaign assets | 14-52 hrs/month |
| Reporting | Build weekly reports from spreadsheets or CRM exports | Use dashboards and KPI views | 13-33 hrs/month |
| CRM updates | Manually create HubSpot contacts, deals, notes, and associations | Sync Excelr8 data into HubSpot | 14-30 hrs/month |
| Newsletter creation | Manually gather files and assemble newsletter inputs | Trigger generation from selected date folders | 10-36 hrs/month |

## Feature-Level Value

### Dashboard

The dashboard reduces time spent preparing manual reporting snapshots. Instead of checking multiple systems or spreadsheets, the team can see lead volume, lead status, lead tiers, and recent activity in one place.

Estimated value:

- Saves 7-15 hours per month
- Improves management visibility
- Helps the team spot pipeline issues faster

### File Ingestion

File ingestion allows CSV lead lists to be uploaded directly into the automation pipeline. This reduces manual formatting, copy-paste work, and inconsistent data entry.

Estimated value:

- Saves 10-20 hours per month
- Reduces spreadsheet errors
- Makes new lead sources easier to activate

### Dossiers

Dossiers give the team a centralized place to review lead research and context. This helps sales or growth users prepare personalized outreach without manually searching across LinkedIn, notes, or external tools.

Estimated value:

- Saves 5-10 minutes per researched lead
- Saves 30-130 hours per month depending on lead volume
- Improves outreach personalization and reply quality

### Post Radar

Post Radar helps identify LinkedIn users who are already engaging with relevant content. Instead of cold prospecting only from static lists, the team can use real engagement signals from comments and reactions.

Estimated value:

- Saves 8-18 hours per month
- Improves lead relevance
- Helps identify warmer prospects based on public engagement

### Campaign Manager

Campaign Manager turns uploaded CSVs into structured campaigns. It normalizes lead fields, creates campaign records, links leads to campaigns, and can support Airtable and n8n workflow creation.

Estimated value:

- Saves 4-8 hours per campaign launch
- Saves 14-52 hours per month for 4-8 campaigns
- Reduces operational dependency on manual setup
- Makes campaign launches more repeatable

### KPI Dashboard

The KPI dashboard gives the team a clearer view of campaign performance without rebuilding reports manually.

Estimated value:

- Saves 6-18 hours per month
- Speeds up campaign reviews
- Helps the team decide what to optimize, pause, or scale

### Newsletter

The newsletter feature helps generate content from date-based files and automation workflows. This makes recurring newsletter production faster and more consistent.

Estimated value:

- Saves 3-6 hours per newsletter
- Saves 10-36 hours per month depending on frequency
- Supports a more consistent publishing workflow

### HubSpot Sync

HubSpot sync moves Excelr8 leads into HubSpot as contacts, campaigns into deals, and adds useful notes such as dossier links and LinkedIn activity.

Estimated value:

- Saves 14-30 hours per month
- Reduces duplicate CRM data entry
- Keeps sales teams working in HubSpot with fresher data
- Improves handoff between automation workflows and sales follow-up

## Overall Business Benefit

Excelr8 creates value in four main ways:

1. It saves operator time by automating repetitive data movement and campaign setup.
2. It improves lead quality by combining enrichment, dossiers, and LinkedIn engagement signals.
3. It improves sales execution by syncing useful context into HubSpot.
4. It improves management visibility through dashboards and KPI reporting.

In practical terms, Excelr8 can save a GTM team an estimated 100-300 hours per month, equal to roughly $50k-$285k per year in operational value depending on lead volume, campaign volume, and team cost.

## Suggested Client-Facing Summary

Excelr8 helps GTM teams move from manual lead operations to automated campaign execution. It centralizes lead data, LinkedIn intelligence, enrichment, campaign setup, CRM syncing, and reporting in one dashboard. For a team processing hundreds or thousands of leads per month, this can save 100-300 hours monthly while improving speed, data quality, and outreach effectiveness.
